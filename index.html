Total serum bilirubin in µmol/litre, at 6-hourly points as in the NICE workbook. Between points the chart joins them with straight lines. Nothing is extended beyond 14 days.

How are the lines worked out?

Born at 23 to 37 weeks. Phototherapy starts at 40 and rises in a straight line to (10 × gestation in weeks − 100) at 72 hours, then stays flat. Exchange transfusion starts at 80 and rises in a straight line to (10 × gestation in weeks) at 72 hours, then stays flat. For example at 36 weeks: phototherapy 260 and exchange 360 from 72 hours.

Born at 38 weeks or more. The workbook holds a typed table: phototherapy rises from 100 at birth to 350 at 96 hours; exchange rises from 100 to 450 at 42 hours. Both then stay flat.

Reading the result. Each reading shows how far above or below each line it sits, in µmol/litre (the reading minus the line at that age). A reading at or above a line counts as on that line. At birth the two lines are both 100 for babies of 38 weeks or more, as in the workbook.

About this tool

This is a web version of the Treatment threshold graphs Excel tool that accompanies the NICE guideline Jaundice in newborn babies under 28 days (CG98, 2010; workbook last saved 2015). The lines are tested against the workbook for every gestational age. Plotting your own readings and the banding of each reading are additions to the workbook.

NICE has since noted that bilirubin assays from different manufacturers give different results, and plans to review thresholds for term babies. Check the current guideline: nice.org.uk/guidance/cg98.

Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere. Refreshing the page clears the readings. Not a NICE product.

This tool supports, and does not replace, clinical judgement and current guidance. It is a reference aid, not a certified medical device. Use at your own discretion.
